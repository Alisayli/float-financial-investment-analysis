Float Financial — Venture Capital Investment Analysis

Independent Investment Research | November 2026

An independent venture capital investment analysis of Float Financial, a Canadian fintech company providing corporate cards, expense management, and financial operating tools.

Project Overview

This project evaluates Float Financial's business model, market opportunity, competitive positioning, financial risks, and potential investor returns.

Deliverables

Investment Committee Memo (PDF)

Investment thesis and recommendation

Market opportunity and competitive analysis

Principal investment and credit risks

Valuation considerations and due diligence priorities

Five-Year Valuation Model (Excel)

Bear, base, and bull revenue forecasts (2027–2031)

Exit valuation and revenue multiple analysis

Investor MOIC and IRR calculations

Ownership dilution and sensitivity analysis

Methodology

The analysis combines publicly available company disclosures, industry research, and illustrative financial assumptions.

Float Financial's actual revenue, profitability, and capitalization table are not publicly available. Financial projections and investor returns are therefore scenario analyses rather than reported company results.
